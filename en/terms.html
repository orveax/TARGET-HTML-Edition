SAMPLE TERMS STRUCTURE

Define the real rules before you publish them.

The eight headings below are the frozen PG31 structure. They identify the topics a buyer may need to address; they do not prescribe final legal wording.

On this page
  1. Site use
  2. Information accuracy
  3. Enquiries
  4. Intellectual property
  5. External links
  6. Liability placeholder
  7. Governing law placeholder
  8. Contact
01

Site use

Describe how visitors may use the production website and what conduct the business actually needs to restrict. The ORIGEX Demo does not assume that merely viewing a page creates a contract with the future buyer.

Production review points

  • Define permitted use in language appropriate to the real website and audience.
  • Add account, ordering or restricted-access rules only if those functions actually exist.
  • Do not copy generic prohibitions that the business cannot reasonably administer or explain.
02

Information accuracy

Explain how product, supplier, market, availability, certification and other business information is maintained on the real website. Demo content is illustrative and cannot be presented as verified production data.

If the production business publishes time-sensitive commercial information, define the appropriate update, verification and correction process rather than promising universal accuracy.

Demo boundary: showing a product, market, document or supplier in ORIGEX does not represent current stock, authorization, commercial representation or verified regulatory status.

03

Enquiries

State what happens when a visitor sends an RFQ, product submission, partner application or general enquiry on the production website. The current template demonstrates interface flows; it does not create acceptance, supply, representation, exclusivity or pricing obligations.

  • Clarify when an enquiry is received and who may respond.
  • Define when a separate quotation, agreement, purchase order or other commercial instrument becomes relevant.
  • Do not treat a Demo confirmation message as evidence that the request has been accepted.
04

Intellectual property

Replace this section with wording that accurately reflects ownership and permitted use of the production website's brand, text, photography, product data, documents and other materials.

The commercial template package itself has separate licensing and asset-credit documentation. A buyer should not infer that third-party trademarks, supplier materials or user-submitted content automatically belong to the website operator.

05

External links

If the production website links to social networks, messaging tools, supplier sites, document hosts or other external destinations, explain their role accurately. The buyer should review which destinations are used and how users are informed that they are leaving the site where appropriate.

Do not imply that the website operator owns, controls, endorses or guarantees a third-party destination merely because a link is present.

06

Liability placeholder

This is intentionally a placeholder. Liability, warranty, exclusion, limitation and indemnity wording can have material legal consequences and may be restricted by applicable law.

Buyer action required: replace this section with reviewed wording appropriate to the real services, users, contracts and jurisdiction. ORIGEX does not supply a default liability cap or universal warranty exclusion.

07

Governing law placeholder

Do not publish a country, state, court, arbitration forum or dispute procedure simply because it appears in a generic template. The correct approach depends on the business entity, users, transaction model and applicable law.

Replace this placeholder after appropriate legal review and keep jurisdiction, venue and dispute wording consistent with the business's real contractual documents.

08

Contact

Replace the Demo contact with the channel the production business actually uses for questions about its website terms. Do not describe a contact as a legal department unless that role genuinely exists.

Current configurable Demo contact: trade@example.com. You can also use the Contact page.
