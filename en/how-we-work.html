How We Work

From information to a decision, then to the right commercial next step.

We start by understanding the company, product and opportunity, review the fit, then define responsibilities and the commercial route before execution. Submitting a product or opening a discussion does not automatically mean acceptance, import or distribution.

Qualification Logic

We review the opportunity from four angles before going deeper.

The aim is not to accept every product. It is to establish whether both sides have a practical basis for a useful next discussion.

Company or Brand

Who is presenting the product, the business context and the commercial background around the opportunity.

Product & Data

Category, origin, pack, storage, shelf-life and available documents where relevant.

Market & Channel

Whether the product and category show an initial fit with the proposed market, channel and opportunity.

Commercial Readiness

Enough information on MOQ, terms, documents and the proposed working arrangement to start a structured discussion.

Four Steps

A concise route from opportunity to a commercial next step.

Share the Opportunity

Send clear information about the company or brand, product, category and the opportunity you want to discuss.

Review the Fit

We review the data, category, market and channel and determine whether there is a practical basis to move forward.

Agree Roles & Route

If the opportunity fits, we define scope, responsibilities and core commercial terms before execution.

Move to the Documented Step

We begin the agreed action, then review continuity or expansion only where actual results and the opportunity support it.

Roles & Responsibilities

Clear responsibility before execution reduces assumptions.

Manufacturer / Supplier / Brand

  • Provide accurate company and product information.
  • Share available documents and certifications where relevant.
  • Clarify packaging, MOQ and available commercial terms.
  • Confirm any commitment through a clear agreement.

ORIGEX Trading Demo

  • Organize information and review initial commercial fit.
  • Identify potential route and channels by category and opportunity.
  • Coordinate the next step within an agreed scope.
  • Avoid advance promises of acceptance, sales or coverage.

Buyer / Market Channel

  • Assess actual need and channel fit.
  • Confirm quantity, timing and requirements when interested.
  • Review its own commercial and regulatory requirements.
  • Make purchasing or listing decisions independently.
Clear commercial boundaries

ORIGEX Trading Demo does not guarantee regulatory approval, market demand, buyer decisions, sales results, exclusivity or agency. Any actual commitment is defined in the appropriate document or agreement before execution.

Required Information

A clearer starting point makes the next decision easier.

Not every document has to be available in the first message, but the core information should be sufficient to understand the product and opportunity without assumptions.

  • Company or Brand
    Business identity and context.
  • Product & Category
    A clear product and use description.
  • Origin, Pack & Storage
    Including shelf life where relevant.
  • MOQ & Available Terms
    Where available for initial review.
  • Certificates & Documents
    Available records only; no unsupported claims.
  • Proposed Opportunity
    Market, channel or expected commercial use.
  • Product Materials
    Brochure, datasheet or other available files.
Decision Flow

The review does not always end with the same outcome.

The initial decision is human and commercial, not an automated acceptance. The outcome only defines the next step.

Proceed to Commercial Discussion

The information is sufficient and there is an initial fit that supports discussing scope, terms and the next action.

Clarify More Information

The opportunity may remain reviewable, but data, documents or commercial terms need clarification first.

Not a Fit at This Stage

There is not enough practical basis to move forward now. This is not presented as a permanent judgment or automated result.
