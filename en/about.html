Who We Are

Trading built around the product, the opportunity and the market route.

The ORIGEX model focuses on selected food categories and structured relationships with manufacturers, brands and suppliers. It starts with company, product and category information, then connects that information to a suitable sales, distribution or market route.

The role is commercial and tied to a real opportunity; it is not a generic consulting service. Starting a discussion or submitting a product does not automatically mean product acceptance, import, distribution or representation.

Commercial Clarity

What does starting a discussion mean, and what does it not mean?

Clear Information

Review starts with company, product, category and opportunity information before a commercial step is taken.

No Automatic Commitment

Submitting a product or starting a discussion does not automatically mean acceptance, import or distribution.

Agree Before Execution

Pricing, quantities, scope and other commercial commitments are defined in the appropriate agreement before execution.

No Agency or Exclusivity Without Documentation

A discussion is not agency, exclusivity or official representation unless it is expressly agreed and documented.
