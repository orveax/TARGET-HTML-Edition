About ORIGEX

A food trading company connecting suitable products with market opportunities.

ORIGEX is a flexible commercial demo for import, distribution and B2B trading companies. It presents how a business works with manufacturers, brands and suppliers, links products to fit review and then to the right market route.

Who We Are

Food trading built around the product, the opportunity and the market.

This demo suits a food trading, import and distribution company focused on selected categories and direct relationships with manufacturers, brands and suppliers.

Commercial Clarity

Clear terms for both sides.

Every discussion starts with clear information about the company, product and opportunity. Commercial commitments begin only after appropriate terms are agreed by both sides.

01INF

Clear Information

Review the company, product, category and opportunity information before taking the next step.

02NAC

No Automatic Commitment

Sending a product or starting a discussion does not automatically mean import, distribution or acceptance.

03AGR

Agree Before Execution

Pricing, quantities, scope and other commercial commitments are defined in the appropriate agreement before execution.

04DOC

No Agency or Exclusivity Without Documentation

No discussion should be understood as agency, exclusivity or official representation unless expressly agreed and documented.
