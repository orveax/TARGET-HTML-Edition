Qualification Criteria

Is the product ready for an initial commercial review?

Readiness here means the information is clear enough to review. It is not a product-quality approval.

Ready for initial review

  • Company identity and role are clear: manufacturer, supplier or brand owner.
  • Product name, category and country of origin/production are stated.
  • Pack size and commercial packaging/case configuration are defined.
  • Storage, shelf life and MOQ approach are available where relevant.
  • Applicable documents or certification references are identified.
  • Target market or channel context is stated when known.

Clarify before submission

  • The product is only an idea with no defined specification or origin.
  • Packaging, storage or shelf-life information is unknown where it materially affects evaluation.
  • Certification or compliance claims are mentioned without a real reference.
  • The request assumes acceptance, exclusivity or distribution rights before review.

Missing information is a readiness issue, not a quality judgment. Complete what can be verified before submitting.

Required Information & Documents

Prepare a practical readiness pack, not a folder of unsupported claims.

The exact regulatory requirements vary by product and market. These are evaluation inputs, not a legal sufficiency checklist.

Company Profile

Who the company is and whether it acts as manufacturer, supplier or brand owner.

Product Datasheet / Specification

Core product information that can be reviewed and replaced with verified buyer data.

Packaging Configuration

Pack size, case configuration and relevant storage or handling information.

Applicable References

Certification or compliance references only when they genuinely exist and apply.

Commercial Baseline

MOQ approach, availability method and other baseline information needed for an initial discussion.

Market / Channel Context

Intended market or sales-channel context when known, clearly presented as an evaluation target.

Commercial Boundaries

Submitting information starts a review route — it does not create commercial rights.

No automatic acceptance

Submission does not imply product acceptance, representation or distribution.

No exclusivity or territory rights

No exclusive relationship or territory right arises from a template submission.

No certificate verification

Certification references shown in the demo are illustrative references, not verification.

No guaranteed market entry

A target-market reference does not mean registration, approval or guaranteed distribution.

All supplier, product and market information in the ORIGEX demo is fictional or illustrative template content and must be replaced with verified business information before publication or commercial use.
