## 9. Arabic RTL Rules
- Reading order: content start at right; media composition may remain visually balanced without naive mirroring.
- Directional actions use arrow-left where meaning is forward in RTL.
- Email, phone, SKU, units and URLs use bidi-safe LTR isolation.
- Product cards keep product data order consistent and readable.
- Mobile: hero content first, media second; CTA stack full width where required.

## 10. English LTR Rules
- Standard LTR reading order.
- Forward actions use arrow-right.
- Mobile: hero content first, media second.
- English copy may require slightly wider CTA labels; buttons must not fixed-width clip.

## 11. Responsive Contract
- 360–412: single-column hero/cards; 20px gutters; full-width hero CTAs; no horizontal scroll; role/product grids stack.
- 768–820: two-column card groups where content remains readable; hero may remain stacked if media pressure is high.
- 1024: split hero active; 2–3 column card compositions according to content family.
- 1280–1536: wide benchmark using 1320 max container; 3–4 column commercial grids.
- 1920: content remains capped; no uncontrolled line length or stretched cards.
