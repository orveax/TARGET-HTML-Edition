# ORIGEX — Image & Media System V1

Product ID: ORX-P01  
Owner: ORVEAX  
Status: APPROVED & FROZEN  
Approval Date: 2026-08-19

## 1. Visual Direction

ORIGEX uses a Natural Industrial Premium image language.

Priority subjects:
- B2B food trading.
- wholesale/distribution.
- warehouses and pallets.
- logistics and loading.
- food manufacturing and packaging lines.
- generic packaged food/products.
- market access and channels.
- ports/containers where contextually useful.

Avoid restaurant-style food glamour, generic corporate handshake imagery, fake-luxury imagery, overprocessed HDR, and irrelevant lifestyle photography.

## 2. Source Policy

Preview sourcing preference:
1. Pexels.
2. Unsplash.
3. Pixabay or other vetted sources only when needed.

Rules:
- Third-party assets are checked at time of acquisition for commercial preview rights and recorded in the Asset Register.
- Third-party stock photography is preview-only by default.
- It is not included in the customer ZIP unless redistribution rights are explicitly verified and documented.
- External source policy can be tightened without reopening page scope.

## 3. Customer ZIP Policy

Default included visuals:
- ORVEAX-created SVG/CSS graphics.
- ORVEAX-created pattern assets.
- neutral placeholders.
- explicitly redistributable assets with documented license.

No TARGET/client photography, logos, brand marks or proprietary supplier assets enter the commercial package.

## 4. Aspect Ratios

- Hero: 3:2 or 16:10.
- Mobile hero/editorial crop: 4:5 where required.
- Product: 1:1 primary.
- Product detail secondary: 4:5 when needed.
- Supplier/brand media: 4:3.
- Blog/editorial: 16:9.
- Case study: 16:10.
- Facility: 3:2.
- Avatar: 1:1 if used.
- Logos: SVG or contained raster inside registered logo frame.

## 5. Media Frames

Registered media behaviors:
- Product Frame.
- Supplier Logo Frame.
- Certification Mark Frame.
- Editorial Image Frame.
- Case Study Frame.
- Facility Frame.
- Map/Location Frame.
- Resource/PDF Thumbnail Frame.

Frames control ratio, background, border, radius, padding, object-fit and responsive behavior centrally.

## 6. Product Photography Rules

- Prefer neutral/light controlled backgrounds.
- Show packaging/product clearly.
- Avoid visible third-party trademarks in generic demo products where practical.
- Product cards use consistent crop and scale.
- No essential product information is embedded only inside an image.

## 7. Hero Composition Rules

- Image must support readable text composition in intended RTL/LTR layout.
- Prefer usable negative space instead of forced overlays.
- Subject placement is reviewed separately for Arabic and English when the crop changes materially.
- Hero media is optimized as high-priority content and is not blindly lazy-loaded.

## 8. People / Trademark Rules

- Environment is generally preferred over posed people.
- Where people are used, imagery should show realistic business/logistics/manufacturing activity.
- Avoid imagery that implies endorsement by an identifiable person or company.
- Avoid prominent third-party logos/trademarks unless specifically justified and cleared.

## 9. Optimization

- WebP preferred for raster delivery; other modern formats may be used where supported by delivery strategy.
- Below-fold media uses lazy loading.
- Width/height attributes provided where practical to reduce layout shift.
- Responsive image sizes used when materially beneficial.
- Preview assets are compressed; multi-megabyte raw photos are prohibited.

## 10. Asset Register — Mandatory Fields

Every third-party or externally sourced asset records:
- Asset ID.
- file name.
- page/section.
- source/provider.
- original source reference.
- creator/photographer when applicable.
- license/status at acquisition.
- acquisition date.
- preview-only YES/NO.
- redistribution verified YES/NO.
- trademark/person risk notes.
- replacement notes.

## 11. Governance

Pages choose media from the approved image/media language. They do not invent new crop systems or asset policies.
