# ORX-P01 — Brand Exploration 01

Status: IN PROGRESS  
Gate: 02 — Demo Brand Identity  
Owner: ORVEAX  
Product: B2B Food Trading, Wholesale, Import–Export & Supplier HTML Template  

## Objective
Define a distinct commercial demo identity before design tokens and final UI production. The demo brand must be independent from TARGET while preserving the mature B2B trading UX direction learned from the TARGET client project.

## Non-negotiables
- Arabic-first product identity.
- English version must remain equally professional.
- Name must be easy to pronounce in Arabic and English.
- Identity must feel B2B trade / supply / market access, not restaurant or grocery retail.
- Identity must work for importer, distributor, wholesaler, manufacturer and supplier scenarios.
- ORVEAX remains the product author and rights owner; the demo brand is not the author brand.
- Avoid using TARGET brand assets, supplier identities, legal copy or client-specific claims.

## Initial naming screen
Several obvious names were screened out because they are already used in relevant or adjacent markets:
- Tawrid — active supply-chain and food-industry brands exist in MENA.
- Mawred — used by trading / food / sourcing businesses.
- Rifada — used by an existing food trading/manufacturing company.
- Vendra / Nuvora / Tradova / Tradara / Tijaro / Qantra — active brands already exist.

## Working Candidate A — MAVRED

### Rationale
MAVRED is an invented, internationalized name inspired by the Arabic concept of “mawred / source / supply” without using the literal generic term. It is short, industrial enough for B2B, and does not push the template toward restaurant, grocery or consumer-food branding.

### Working Arabic rendering
مافريد

This Arabic spelling is provisional and requires visual/phonetic review before lock.

### Positioning line
B2B Food Trading · Wholesale · Import–Export · Suppliers

### Visual direction A — Trade & Origin
- Character: premium, commercial, structured, international.
- Avoid: organic-farm clichés, restaurant aesthetics, generic corporate blue-only styling.
- Core visual language: route, origin, cargo, sourcing, packaging, market access, product documentation.
- Logo direction: strong wordmark + compact abstract mark derived from route / source / linked nodes.
- Geometry: controlled radius, crisp borders, restrained shadows.
- Photography: manufacturing, packaging, palletized goods, warehouses, ports, product closeups, trade meetings.
