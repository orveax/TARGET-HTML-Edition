# ORIGEX — Mini Brand Guide V1

**Identity Direction:** Route 3 — Premium Mark  
**Product:** ORX-P01  
**Owner:** ORVEAX  
**Use case:** Website, ThemeForest demo, product documentation and marketplace assets.

## Brand idea

**ORIGEX** represents a clearer commercial route from source to market.

The visual identity combines:
- a simplified global-market globe;
- a source-to-market route;
- an upward opportunity arrow;
- premium B2B typography;
- restrained trade-oriented colors.

### Usage hierarchy
1. Use the primary English horizontal logo as the default product mark.
2. Use the Arabic-facing lockup in Arabic-first presentations or localized surfaces where a full Arabic expression is required.
3. Use the bilingual lockup only when Arabic and English need equal visibility.
4. Use the mark alone for favicon, compact UI, badges and small identity surfaces.
5. Use the light logo on Deep Ink/dark approved backgrounds.

## Brand colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Trade Ink | `#15343B` | Primary logo and headings |
| Deep Ink | `#0D252B` | Dark backgrounds / maximum contrast |
| Route Teal | `#3F6F68` | Supporting identity and route system |
| Origin Copper | `#C47A4A` | Route, node and opportunity accent |
| Warm Sand | `#E8DFD0` | Supporting light accent |
| Base | `#FAF8F4` | Main light background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Surface / reverse use |

**Rule:** Copper is an accent. Do not turn it into the dominant page color.

## Typography

### Arabic
**Tajawal**  
Use 700–800 for major headings and identity-facing Arabic copy; 400–500 for supporting text.

### English
**Manrope**  
Use 700–800 for headings and product identity; 400–600 for UI and supporting content.

## Brand background

**Asset:** `assets/brand/origex-brand-field.svg`

Use it for:
- Hero side fields;
- premium section backgrounds;
- ThemeForest preview frames;
- documentation covers;
- presentation/title surfaces.

Keep it subtle. Route lines and market nodes should support hierarchy, not compete with content.

## Pattern relationship

ORIGEX already contains six system patterns:
- PT01 Route Lines
- PT02 Trade Grid
- PT03 Dot Matrix
- PT04 Market Nodes
- PT05 Packaging Geometry
- PT06 Flow Lines

The Route 3 identity does **not** replace these patterns. The logo/mark is the identity layer; PT01–PT06 remain the reusable website pattern layer.

## Clear space

Keep a minimum clear area equal to approximately two origin-node diameters around the mark and logo lockups.

Do not crowd the logo with:
- navigation labels;
- supplier marks;
- page borders;
- decorative nodes;
- route-line backgrounds.

## Minimum digital size

- Mark: 24 px minimum / 32 px preferred.
- Horizontal logo: 132 px minimum.
- Bilingual logo: 220 px minimum.

## Correct use

Use:
- Trade Ink logo on Base/white surfaces;
- Light logo on Deep Ink/dark surfaces;
- Copper only for route and directional accents;
- fixed non-mirrored symbol geometry in both RTL and LTR.

## Incorrect use

Do not:
- stretch, rotate or mirror the logo;
- redraw the arrow separately;
- use unapproved colors;
- add glow, bevel, shadow or 3D effects;
- place the full-color logo on noisy photography without a controlled surface;
- mix the ORIGEX mark with supplier/customer logos;
- use the symbol as a generic cart, delivery or restaurant icon.

## Tone of visual expression

ORIGEX should feel:
- premium but not luxury-fashion;
- global but not generic-corporate;
- trade-oriented but not industrial-heavy;
- modern but not startup-gimmicky;
- structured, credible and commercially useful.

## Website rule

The new Route 3 identity must be integrated centrally through the global brand/system layer after approval. Do not patch individual pages with different logo files or ad-hoc colors.

## Canonical positioning

**Arabic:** من المصدر إلى السوق، بمسار تجاري أوضح.  
**English:** From source to market, through a clearer commercial route.
