## 1. Positioning

ORIGEX is a premium Arabic-first bilingual HTML template for B2B food trading, import/export, wholesale, distribution, manufacturers, suppliers and market access.

The visual language communicates:
- source and origin;
- trade routes and movement;
- market access;
- supply-chain confidence;
- product credibility;
- international B2B professionalism.

It must not drift into restaurant, grocery-shop, organic-farm or generic corporate-template aesthetics.

## 2. Language Priority

- Arabic is the master commercial language and RTL experience.
- English is a complete professional LTR adaptation.
- AR and EN receive independent typography, spacing, direction, mixed-data and responsive QA.
- Language completeness is a release requirement, not an optional demo state.

## 3. Logo Direction — Origin Route Mark

The ORIGEX mark combines:
1. **Origin** — source/start point.
2. **Route** — movement from source toward market.
3. **Exchange / X** — trade/export/exchange geometry.

Rules:
- geometric and SVG-first;
- survives monochrome use;
- no wheat/fork/spoon/cart/globe/container clichés;
- symbol may work independently as a mark/favicon;
- public wordmark is ORIGEX; `أوريجكس` is supporting language rendering, not a second identity.

Approved active brand assets are tracked in `ASSET-LICENSE-REGISTER-V1.md`.

## 8. Image Direction

Authority: `IMAGE-MEDIA-SYSTEM-V1.md`.

Use B2B trade imagery focused on:
- manufacturing/packing;
- products in commercial context;
- warehouse/pallet/carton activity selectively;
- supplier/product review;
- distribution/market channels;
- sourcing/export context.

Avoid:
- restaurant plated-food clichés;
- excessive produce flat-lays;
- generic handshake stock;
- fake dashboards;
- client-owned TARGET imagery/assets;
- prominent unlicensed third-party brand marks.

## 9. Visual Personality

**Natural Industrial Premium / Origin → Route → Market**

- restrained radius and elevation;
- borders/surfaces do most structural work;
- strong whitespace and editorial rhythm;
- high information clarity;
- premium but operational rather than decorative;
- motion is subtle and reduced-motion safe;
- Arabic is deliberately composed, not mechanically mirrored.
